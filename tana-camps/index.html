Elephants in Africa

Elephant

7 Minutes

The elephant most safari-goers know is the African bush elephant, a separate species from the smaller forest elephant. It is social, intelligent and long-lived, and a keystone species: by felling trees, digging for water and spreading seeds over great distances, elephants shape the savanna and keep it open for countless other animals.
